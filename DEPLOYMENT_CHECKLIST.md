**A. Confirm Signup Email**

- Subject: Confirm your email for Gisenyi Gadgets
- Make sure "Confirm signup" is enabled

**B. Magic Link Email** 
- Template Name: Magic Link
- Subject: Your magic link for Gisenyi Gadgets

**C. Reset Password Email** ⚠️ CRITICAL FOR FORGOT PASSWORD
- Template Name: Reset Password
- Subject: Reset your password - Gisenyi Gadgets
- Redirect URL should include: `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}`
- Make sure it redirects to: `com.gisenyigadgets.app://reset-password`

**D. Email Change Confirmation**
- Template Name: Change Email Address
- Subject: Confirm your new email
